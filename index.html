Material Science

Know Your Grade

From budget-friendly 201 to marine-grade 316 — we stock the full stainless spectrum.

201
Economic

Cost-effective chromium-manganese steel for indoor & light-duty fastening.

202
Versatile

Balanced strength and corrosion resistance — the workhorse of general fabrication.

304
Industrial

The 18/8 classic. Excellent corrosion resistance for chemical, food & structural use.

316
Marine

Molybdenum-enriched for salt water, chlorides & pharma — maximum protection.
